COOKIE POLICY

Which cookies we use and how you can manage your settings.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, work in the same way. Under the Slovenian Electronic Communications Act (ZEKom-2) strictly necessary cookies do not require consent; for all others we always ask for consent in advance.

2. Which cookies we use

Our website is simple: we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking. The only third-party content that sets cookies is the embedded Google Maps, which is loaded only once you explicitly allow it.

3. External files without cookies

Fonts and icons are loaded from Google Fonts and the cdnjs/Cloudflare, unpkg and jsDelivr networks. These providers do not set cookies but do receive your IP address, which is technically required to deliver the files. See our privacy policy.

You can change your choice at any time with the button below or the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page:
